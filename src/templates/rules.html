Main rules

  • A carried competitor under the minimum weight wears a backpack that makes up the difference.
  • Dropping the person you carry has no penalty, but they must be picked up immediately.
  • Spiked heels, hiking boots and football boots are banned. Footwear is inspected before the race.
  • A written protest must be handed in within 15 minutes of the last pair finishing. The fee is €150.
