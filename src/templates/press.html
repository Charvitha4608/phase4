Media and Accreditation

Media representatives apply for accreditation through the Wife Carrying office.

Include in your application

  • Name of the media outlet, your name and job title
  • Full address, phone number and email
  • Special requests, such as network connection, work space or parking
  • Whether you plan to use a drone
  • Arrival date and time, and the names of everyone in your group

Send to toimisto@eukonkanto.fi
